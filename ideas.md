# OIshiinori Landing Page — Design Direction

## Ground-truth reference

This page is a close recreation of the supplied tall Japanese sushi restaurant landing-page reference. The reference controls the composition, section order, dense editorial rhythm, rounded black navigation bar, cream paper background, vermilion red accents, menu grid, image-led middle story section, contact form, and dark footer. The OIshiinori logo supplied by the user is the brand source of truth and replaces the reference brand mark.

## Chosen direction: Vermilion Paper Menu

### Design Movement
Japanese editorial menu design blended with contemporary restaurant identity systems and tactile print ephemera.

### Core Principles
1. Preserve the reference's long-scroll poster-like composition and visual cadence.
2. Use warm paper, charcoal ink, and vermilion as the main emotional vocabulary.
3. Balance dense information blocks with generous cream space and food photography.
4. Make every interactive element feel like a physical menu label: crisp, tactile, and easy to scan.

### Color Philosophy
The warm ivory background should feel like uncoated menu stock. Charcoal provides the grounding ink color and supports legibility. Vermilion-red is the ownable action color: it signals heat, appetite, and Japanese lacquer without relying on gradients. Muted sage and peach may appear only as quiet supporting accents in photography and decorative blocks.

### Layout Paradigm
A single-column narrative poster with deliberate asymmetry inside each band: offset hero copy, overlapping food imagery, tab-like category navigation, six-item menu grid, editorial split blocks, then a compact contact panel and dark footer. On mobile, the same sequence remains intact and becomes a vertical menu poster rather than a generic card stack.

### Signature Elements
- Thick charcoal pill navigation with a circular logo medallion interrupting the bar.
- Vermilion price strips and small circular “fresh” badges on menu cards.
- Geometric translucent red paper blocks and hand-drawn wave lines as section punctuation.

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel immediate and physical. Category pills filter the visible menu without changing the page's structure. Buttons use short press feedback, underlines, and red fill transitions. Forms acknowledge submission inline rather than navigating away.

### Animation
Use short 180–260ms ease-out transitions for pills, menu cards, and form states. Fade and translate the menu cards by small distances only. Decorative wave lines remain still or drift imperceptibly. Respect reduced-motion preferences and avoid animation that changes layout.

### Typography System
Use a condensed display face for the oversized wordmark-like headings and section labels, paired with a warm readable sans-serif for descriptions and controls. Headings are mostly uppercase or compact title case, with occasional Japanese kana/kanji accents as graphic texture. Prices are large, bold, and aligned to the red strip for quick scanning.

### Brand Essence
OIshiinori is the neighborhood Japanese kitchen for people who want sushi, ramen, snacks, and cafe drinks in one easygoing stop. Personality: **playful, generous, graphic**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are brief and appetizing. CTAs are direct and lightly playful. Microcopy feels human, not corporate.

Example lines:
- “ROLL IN HUNGRY.”
- “A LITTLE CRUNCH. A LOT OF OISHII.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use the supplied circular OIshiinori logo prominently as the center medallion in the navigation and as a larger identity mark in the hero. Pair it with a custom text lockup using condensed display typography rather than recreating the logo as plain body text.

### Signature Brand Color
**OIshiinori Vermilion — #D9361E**, a vivid lacquer red used for prices, buttons, badges, and the occasional geometric paper block.

## Style Decisions

The page will prioritize fidelity to the supplied reference over generic landing-page conventions. It will not use a purple gradient, excessive rounded cards, or a centered hero-only layout. It will use the actual menu-derived offerings and avoid invented customer reviews or testimonials.
